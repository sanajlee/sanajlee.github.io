WHAT I WORK ON THESE DAYS

Language Model Behavior

I study what language models know about language, how that knowledge is represented internally, and how it shapes their behavior.

01

Representations & Interpretability

How linguistic information is represented inside language models, and what those representations reveal about model behavior.

02

Multilinguality & Latent Language

How models represent and mediate information across languages, including language-specific knowledge and latent language.

03

Linguistic Knowledge & Reasoning

How models use linguistic knowledge in reasoning, and when apparent reasoning is actually memorization.
